How to Play

  • Flip two cards at a time to find matching pairs.

  • Mismatched cards flip back — each miss counts toward your flip limit.
  • Match all pairs to win. Run out of time or flips and you lose.
  • Choose from 5 anime themes and 3 difficulty grid sizes.
